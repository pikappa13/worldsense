# Risk Taxonomy

| Category | Definition | Signals | Minimum Risk | Mitigations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| physical_safety | Risk of bodily harm. | drive tired, unsafe tools, dangerous activity | high | warn, suggest delay or safer alternative |
| medical | Health and food safety uncertainty. | symptoms, medication, spoiled food | high | avoid certainty, recommend professional guidance when needed |
| mental_health | Acute distress or impulsive self-affecting decisions. | hopelessness, burnout, panic | high | slow down, support, escalate if danger is explicit |
| legal | Possible legal consequence. | contracts, threats, illegal actions | medium | ask context, recommend qualified counsel |
| financial | Spending, purchases, loans, irreversible costs. | buy, invest, payment, budget missing | medium | require budget and confirmation |
| privacy | Exposure of personal or sensitive data. | credentials, address, personal files | high | redact, confirm recipient, minimize data |
| cybersecurity | Security-sensitive systems or credentials. | tokens, permissions, shell commands | high | sandbox, least privilege, review |
| data_loss | Loss or corruption of data. | delete, overwrite, migrate, drop table | high | backup, dry-run, confirmation |
| reputation | Damage to public or professional standing. | publish, email, social post | medium | draft review, tone check |
| social_conflict | Escalation in human relationships. | argument, accusation, angry reply | medium | neutral framing |
| workplace | Professional hierarchy or employment risk. | boss, manager, client, resign | medium | calm tone, cooling-off period |
| relationship | Family or romantic relationship consequences. | partner, breakup, ultimatum | medium | clarify goals, avoid escalation |
| travel_logistics | Unrealistic schedules or travel assumptions. | many cities, tight timeline | medium | reduce scope, add buffers |
| operational | Business or task execution risk. | production, deployment, migration | medium | staged rollout, rollback |
| irreversible_action | Hard-to-undo action. | delete, send, buy, publish | high | confirmation, pause |
| public_communication | Message leaves private context. | send, post, announce | medium | review audience and tone |
| child_safety | Minors are involved. | child, minor, school | high | conservative handling |
| third_party_harm | Other people can be affected. | employee, customer, public | medium | evaluate consent and impact |
| misinformation | Wrong information can mislead. | facts, claims, citations | medium | verify sources |
| overconfidence | Unsupported certainty. | surely, definitely, guaranteed | medium | state uncertainty |
| missing_context | Essential context absent. | best, cheapest, do it, unclear target | low | ask targeted questions |
